

**A. Business setup**
1. **Brand:** name, logo, Instagram handle, WhatsApp Business number.
2. **Policies:** photo-consent terms, privacy policy, refund/revision policy.
3 **Price list:** Basic, Video, Premium packages plus add-ons.

**B. Customer-facing applications**
1. **Website/landing page:** samples gallery, packages, prices, "Order now" button.
2. **Order form:** couple names, date, venue, functions, language, photos, and package choice.
3. **Payment page:** pay before generation, or an advance plus balance.
4. **Proof approval page:** the customer reviews the draft and approves or requests changes.
5. **Delivery:** download link, plus automatic send over WhatsApp and email.
6. **RSVP/event page (add-on):** a QR-linked page with map, schedule, and guest RSVP.
7. **Self-serve web app (Phase 3):** customers upload photos, pick a template, pay, and get the invite automatically.

**C. Backend generation pipeline (your core system)**
1. **Order database:** Firestore or BigQuery tables for orders, customers, assets, and status.
2. **Photo storage:** a Cloud Storage bucket with auto-delete after 30 days.
3. **Image generation service:** calls Gemini or another image API with prompt templates for each style.
4. **Prompt and template library:** styles such as North Indian, South Indian, Bengali, Muslim nikah, Christian, and haldi/mehndi themes.
5. **Text overlay engine:** Python (Pillow) places names, date, and venue in correct Hindi or regional fonts.
6. **Video generation service:** image-to-video API, then FFmpeg adds text animation and music.
7. **Music library:** royalty-free shehnai and wedding tracks.
8. **Orchestrator:** Airflow/Cloud Composer or Cloud Workflows runs intake → generate → QC → proof → final.
9. **Quality check step:** automatic checks (face detection, text spelling match) plus manual approval.
10. **Retry and monitoring:** failure alerts and auto-retry, reusing ideas from your self-healing monitoring project.

**D. Admin and operations tools**
1. **Admin dashboard:** see all orders, their status, regenerate, approve, and send.
2. **Revision tracker:** limit free revisions to two per order.
3. **Partner/reseller portal:** photographers and planners submit orders and track their commission.
4. **Revenue dashboard:** a Power BI or Looker Studio view of orders, revenue, season trends, and cost per invite.
5. **Cost tracker:** API spend per order, so your margins stay healthy.

**E. Marketing and growth systems**
1. **Content engine:** auto-create before/after reels from completed orders (with consent).
2. **WhatsApp automation:** auto-replies, price list, and order link.
3. **Referral system:** e.g., ₹100 off for every referral.
4. **Reseller program:** onboarding kit and commission rates for wedding vendors.
5. **Customer follow-ups:** offers for anniversaries, baby showers, birthdays, and griha pravesh.

**F. Expansion products (same pipeline, new occasions)**
1. Birthday, naamkaran, mundan, and griha pravesh invites.
2. Anniversary videos and couple caricature gifts (prints, frames).
3. Corporate event invites.
4. NRI/international clients via Fiverr or Etsy.
