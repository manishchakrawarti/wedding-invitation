## 7. Free advertising playbook

**Every invitation already advertises the site.** Free cards carry "Made with love · Invite All", guest pages link
back, and the selfie frame shows invite-all.com on every status it's posted to. So every customer brings the next
ones. Help it along:

1. **WhatsApp (your strongest channel in India)**
   - Post a status daily: a sample card, a Reel, or a customer's card (with permission).
   - Share **once** in your family, friends, society and office groups with a personal note, e.g. *"I built this,
     please try it for your next function and tell me what you think 🙏"*.
   - Grow the WhatsApp Channel: post new designs and festival cards there.
2. **Partner with wedding vendors (free, very effective):** mehndi artists, photographers, decorators, caterers,
   pandits and banquet halls meet families every day. Send them a sample and offer **₹100 off for their clients**
   with a referral code (already built in: every order gets one). Their clients get a discount, and you get customers.
3. **Facebook groups:** join city groups (*"Pune wedding vendors"*, *"Delhi brides"*, housing-society and
   NRI groups). Read the rules, then share something useful (*"Free tool I made for Hindi wedding cards"*), not ads.
   One post per group.
4. **Instagram:** comment helpfully on wedding planners' and mehndi artists' posts, use 5–10 specific hashtags per
   post, and collaborate with small creators (they post their own card made on Invite All; you share it).
5. **Quora and Reddit:** answer questions like *"How to make a wedding invitation card online for free?"* or
   *"Griha Pravesh invitation message in Hindi"* honestly, with the relevant landing-page link.
6. **Google Business Profile** (free): a "service area business" called Invite All, category *Invitation printing
   service*, with the website and WhatsApp. It helps people searching "Invite All" find you.
7. **Ride the season:**
   - November to February: wedding season.
   - January: Lohri and Makar Sankranti.
   - Holi, Eid, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri and Diwali: make one festival post or Reel a week before each.
   - Griha Pravesh muhurat dates: share them as posts.

**Don't:** buy followers, spam the same message in many groups (WhatsApp and Facebook ban for it), or use other
brands' logos or celebrity photos.

**Later, if you want paid ads:** boost your best-performing Reel on Instagram for ₹100–200/day to brides and
grooms aged 22–35 in 2–3 cities, and stop if it doesn't bring orders within a week.
