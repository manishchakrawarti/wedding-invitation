# Content calendar: November 2026 to January 2027

**3 posts a week:** a Reel on Monday, an image on Wednesday, a tip or wording post on Friday. Plus a daily
WhatsApp status. Images are in [`assets/`](assets/); Reel scripts are in [SOCIAL_KIT.md](SOCIAL_KIT.md), section 4.

**Always use these tagged links,** so the admin **Stats** page (invite-all.com/admin/stats) shows which platform
brings visitors and orders:

| Where | Link to use |
|---|---|
| Instagram bio | `https://invite-all.com/?utm_source=instagram` |
| Facebook posts and Page | `https://invite-all.com/?utm_source=facebook` |
| YouTube descriptions | `https://invite-all.com/?utm_source=youtube` |
| WhatsApp status and groups | `https://invite-all.com/?utm_source=whatsapp` |
| Pinterest pins | the landing page + `?utm_source=pinterest` |
| X | `https://invite-all.com/?utm_source=x` |

Hindi audience? Add `&ui=hi` (e.g. `https://invite-all.com/?utm_source=whatsapp&ui=hi`) and the site opens in Hindi.

## The season at a glance

| Weeks | Theme | Why now |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 (Nov) | **Wedding season** | Peak shaadi months: November to February |
| 5–6 (early Dec) | **Personal cards and the selfie frame** | Guests share them, and every share is free advertising |
| 7–8 (mid–late Dec) | **Christmas, New Year and kitty parties** | Party invitations |
| 9–10 (early Jan) | **Lohri, Makar Sankranti and Griha Pravesh** | Festivals; many families do house-warmings after Sankranti |
| 11–12 (late Jan) | **Wedding wording, gallery, and real customers' cards** | Trust and proof as wedding dates get close |

- **WhatsApp status:** one image or Reel from above.

- **Comment** helpfully on 5 posts by wedding planners, mehndi artists or photographers in your city.

- Open **/admin/stats**: which source brought the most visitors and orders? Do more of that.
- Message 3 local vendors (mehndi, photographers, pandits) with a sample and the ₹100-off referral offer.
- Pin 5 landing pages on Pinterest (list in SOCIAL_KIT.md, section 6).
