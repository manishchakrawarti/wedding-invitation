Questions families ask

Which occasions can I make invitations for?

Weddings plus {{ occasion_types|length }} other occasions: birthdays, anniversaries, pujas (Satyanarayan, Griha Pravesh, Diwali…), baby ceremonies, festivals and parties, and business events. See them all.

Is it really free?

Yes. Creating and previewing is free, and the Standard (720p) download is free. Pay only if you want Full HD (₹9), 2K (₹49) or 4K (₹99), which also makes your invite page live for RSVPs.

How do guests RSVP?

Share your invite page link on WhatsApp. Guests open it, see the schedule and map, add it to their calendar and reply in a tap. You see every reply on your order page.

How fast will I get my invitation?

Self-made invitations are ready in seconds. Done-for-you wedding proofs arrive within 48 hours of payment (24 hours with Premium or Express).

Can the card be in Hindi or my regional language?

Yes: {{ catalog.LANGUAGES.values()|map(attribute='name')|join(', ') }}. Just write the names in your script on the order form, exactly as you want them printed.

Are my photos safe?

Photos are used only to make your invitation, never shown publicly without your separate permission, and deleted automatically within {{ settings.photo_retention_days }} days. See our privacy policy.

What if I don't like the design?

Self-made: switch styles and create another version in seconds, free. Done-for-you: you get {{ settings.max_free_revisions }} free revisions. Tell us what to change and we redesign it. Extra revisions are ₹{{ catalog.ADDONS['extra_revision'].price }} each.

Do you make printed cards?

We deliver print-ready HD files that any local press can print. Message us on WhatsApp for printing help.
